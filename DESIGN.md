# DESIGN.md - Ngopi Sama Kamu

## Identity & Concept
- **Brand Name:** Ngopi Sama Kamu
- **Tagline:** Tempat pulang sejenak, ngobrol santai, dan nikmati secangkir kopi hangat.
- **Mood / Character:** Hangat, bersahabat, santai, unhurried (tidak terburu-buru), otentik, nyaman (cozy).

## Dials
- **ENERGY:** 2 (Warm, relaxed, inviting, human)
- **RHYTHM:** 2 (Organic, comfortable rhythm with purposeful white space, varied section flow)
- **MOTION:** 1 (Subtle interactive hover/focus transitions only, zero looping/distracting animations)

## Color Palette
- **Background Base:** Warm Cream / Oatmeal `#FAF7F2` and `#F3EFE6`
- **Surface / Card Base:** Crisp Clean Warm White `#FFFFFF` with soft warm border `#E6DFD5`
- **Primary Typography:** Deep Roasted Espresso `#1C1512` (WCAG AAA contrast > 14:1 against cream background)
- **Secondary Typography:** Muted Roasted Bean `#5C5046` (WCAG AA contrast > 5.5:1 against cream background)
- **Primary Accent:** Warm Caramel / Terracotta `#BC5B38` (used deliberately for key CTAs and highlights)
- **Secondary Accent:** Soft Forest Sage `#4D624C` / background badge `#EAF0E9` (for fresh/artisan indicators)

## Typography
- **Display / Headings:** `Fraunces` (Google Fonts / Bunny) - soft, relaxed, rounded-serif with warm artisan cafe soul.
- **Body / Interface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` - human, clean, approachable, highly legible Indonesian typography standard.

## Layout & Content Principles
- Simple, clean, and immediately understandable.
- Zero AI buzzwords: no "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", or fabricated statistics.
- Honest, clear sections:
  1. Header with direct navigation and working order/WA CTA.
  2. Hero: welcoming greeting, real opening hours, and clear invitation.
  3. Menu Pilihan (Signature Drinks & Treats) with honest prices, clear taste notes, and category filters.
  4. Suasana & Cerita: What makes "Ngopi Sama Kamu" special (tempat kerja santai, colokan & Wi-Fi siap, playlist santai, biji kopi lokal pilihan).
  5. Info Kunjungan: Lokasi, jam operasional, peta/petunjuk arah, dan tombol kontak WhatsApp langsung.
  6. Footer: Navigasi ringkas, info media sosial nyata, dan jam buka.
